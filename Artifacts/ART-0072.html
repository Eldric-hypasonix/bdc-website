DESCRIPTION:

ART-0072 is a circular bronze mirror measuring approximately 45 centimeters in diameter. The artifact dates back to the late Eastern Han Dynasty, featuring intricate engravings of stylized Chinese dragons along its reverse trim. Despite its age, the bronze surface exhibits no patina or tarnish, maintaining a highly reflective quality.

The anomalous properties of ART-0072 manifest when any conscious human subject looks directly into the reflection. Instead of displaying the user’s current reality, the surface shifts to depict a highly detailed, real-time video sequence of the viewer's ultimate death. The duration of the reflection matches the length of time the subject maintains eye contact, providing progressive detail regarding the location, date, and physical cause of termination.

Testing has confirmed that the events shown by ART-0072 are █████% accurate. Attempts by subjects to intentionally alter their behavior or avoid the circumstances shown in the mirror have invariably resulted in a series of coincidences that ultimately fulfill the original reflection. Psychological evaluations show that 87% of individuals who view their reflection experience severe existential dread, chronic paranoia, and eventual treatment-resistant depression.
