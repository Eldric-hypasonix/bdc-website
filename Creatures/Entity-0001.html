ITEM #: Entity-0001

SECURITY CLEARANCE: LEVEL 3 (RESTRICTED)

OBJECT CLASS: EUCLID

SPECIAL CONTAINMENT PROCEDURES:

Entity-0001 is to be stored in a vacuum-sealed, soundproofed glass display case at Site-19, Sector-4. The display case must be suspended by synthetic polymers exactly 1.5 meters from the floor and walls to mitigate vibration transfer. Under no circumstances is any staff member to enter the containment cell without wearing active, military-grade noise-canceling headphones.

Personnel reporting auditory hallucinations, phantom whispers, or an intense desire to touch their own face must be immediately removed from the sector and subjected to Class-A amnestic therapy. Any testing involving human subjects must be approved by at least two (2) Level 4 researchers.

DESCRIPTION:

Entity-0001 is a porcelain theatrical mask resembling an idealized human face, showing no makers' marks or historical identifiers. The anomaly is physically inert but possesses a highly invasive cognitive hazard. When a sentient subject comes within a 3-meter radius of Entity-0001, they will begin to hear a faint, rhythmic scratching sound, followed by distinct whispering.

The whispers are subjective, always tailored specifically to the listener's deepest personal regrets, insecurities, or forgotten traumatic childhood memories. Over prolonged exposure (exceeding ██ minutes), the subject will become entirely convinced that their own skin is "artificial" or "borrowed." Stage 3 exposure leads to severe self-mutilation, as subjects attempt to "peel away the falsehood" to find what the voice is describing.

If a subject manages to physically wear Entity-0001, the mask permanently bonds to the epidermis via microscopic, root-like calcified filaments. The host's vocal cords will immediately deteriorate, and they will only be able to speak in the collective voices of all previous victims.

ADDENDUM Entity-0001-A: DISCOVERY LOG

Entity-0001 was recovered on 10/14/1998 from an abandoned nineteenth-century opera house in ███████, France. Local authorities were alerted after a group of local urban explorers disappeared. Upon Foundation intervention, recovery teams discovered three bodies inside the dressing rooms. All three individuals had successfully removed their own facial tissue using blunt stage props.

The fourth individual, later designated Subject 0001-01, was found sitting center stage wearing Entity-0001. The subject was completely unresponsive to external stimuli but continuously whispered detailed biographical information regarding the recovery team's commanding officer, Agent ██████, including secrets known only to him. Subject 0001-01 expired during transport due to acute shock; the mask was safely detached post-mortem.
