ITEM #: LOC-0036

SECURITY CLEARANCE: LEVEL 4 (SECRET)

OBJECT CLASS: KETER

SPECIAL CONTAINMENT PROCEDURES:

The five-story residential building housing LOC-0036 in Dagon Township, Yangon, Myanmar, has been completely acquired by Foundation front company "Yangon Urban Renewal Initiative." The exterior windows and primary entrance of the structure have been reinforced with solid steel plating and concrete masonry. The site is permanently designated as Provisional Outpost-89, disguised as an active structural hazard demolition zone.

Under no circumstances is any personnel to step past the threshold of the building's central concrete stairwell without authorization from the Site Director. Any approved exploration teams entering LOC-0036 must be securely fastened to a high-tensile, motorized steel winch tether. Radio contact must be maintained constantly; if a temporal desynchronization event exceeds forty-five (45) seconds, the automatic winch must be activated immediately to retrieve personnel.

DESCRIPTION:

LOC-0036 is a concrete, mid-twentieth-century stairwell located in the interior core of an unnumbered apartment complex. While the building's exterior stands at a standard height of five stories, the internal architectural layout of the stairwell is spatially infinite. Subjects descending or ascending past the landing of the third floor will find that the stairwell continues endlessly in both directions, completely breaking normal spatial constraints.

Furthermore, LOC-0036 possesses a localized temporal distortion field. As individuals descend deeper into the anomaly, linear time breaks down, collapsing into localized, repeating three-minute loops. Subjects caught in these loops are typically unaware of the repetition, though they will occasionally catch glimpses of their own past or future duplicates moving on the flights of stairs immediately above or below them. Physical objects or messages left on the stairs disappear upon the termination of each temporal cycle.

INCIDENT LOG LOC-0036-OMICI

Date: 06/06/2026
Subject: Dr. H. Naing, Lead Temporal Researcher
Log: Dr. Naing entered LOC-0036 during an authorized baseline mapping exercise, equipped with a standard heavy-duty safety tether. At approximately 42 meters deep (estimated 14 flights down), the mechanical steel tether experienced a sudden, instantaneous slackening. When reeled back to the surface, the end of the cable appeared cleanly severed, but showed severe metal fatigue consistent with over eighty (80) years of environmental rust, despite having only been deployed for twelve minutes.

Audio logs from Dr. Naing’s personal body-recorder remained active for an additional seven hours before dropping signal. The following transcript is an excerpt from the final broadcast:

"This is flight 412. Or maybe 413. The concrete patterns are identical. I passed myself four flights back... he was checking his watch, just like I am right now. I shouted at him, but he only heard a whisper. The watch says it's 19:58. It has said it's 19:58 for three lifetimes. Every time I reach the bottom of this flight, I hear my own footsteps starting down the top of the next flight. I am chasing myself down, but I am also running away from me. Don't send anyone down. The line didn't snap. It just grew too old to hold on."

Dr. Naing has not been recovered. Subsequent remote camera deployments have occasionally captured video frames of a severely aged individual matching his description, frozen mid-stride on the lower stairs, disappearing every three minutes.
